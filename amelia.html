Archana Basu, PhD

Assistant Professor, Harvard Medical School
Staff Psychologist, MGH

Program Director
Founding Co-Director, IPC Program

Dr. Basu directs the program’s research and clinical portfolio, using intergenerational and life-course approaches to examine biobehavioral mechanisms linking trauma exposure to children’s mental, physical, and relational health outcomes, and the role of protective factors such as caregiving sensitivity.

Read full bio

Dr. Basu directs the program’s research and clinical portfolio, using intergenerational and life-course approaches to examine biobehavioral mechanisms linking trauma exposure to children’s mental, physical, and relational health outcomes, and the role of protective factors such as caregiving sensitivity. She collaborates on developing, evaluating, and implementing trauma-informed parenting and family-centered psychotherapies, including Dialectical Behavior Therapy-Elementary (DBT-E) and the Parenting Young Children groups. She co-directs the IPC program, a population mental health implementation model in primary care. As a licensed clinical psychologist, she works with children and families coping with trauma, bereavement, and adversities.

Mary Wallace holds an AB in Psychology (Cognitive Neuroscience and Evolutionary Psychology track) from Harvard University.
